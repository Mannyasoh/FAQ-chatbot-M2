HR SAAS COMPANY COMPREHENSIVE FAQ GUIDE

EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING AND POLICIES

Q: What documents do I need to provide for onboarding?
A: New employees must provide a valid government-issued ID, Social Security card or work authorization document, completed I-9 form, direct deposit information, emergency contact details, and signed company handbook acknowledgment. If you're a remote employee, you'll also need proof of your home office setup and internet connectivity requirements. All documents must be submitted within 3 business days of your start date.

Q: How long is the probationary period for new employees?
A: The standard probationary period is 90 days for all full-time employees and 60 days for part-time employees. During this period, your performance will be evaluated at 30, 60, and 90-day intervals. Successful completion of the probationary period is required for full benefit eligibility and job security protection under company policies.

Q: What is the company dress code policy?
A: We maintain a business casual dress code for office workers and smart casual for remote employees during video calls. This includes collared shirts, dress pants or khakis, closed-toe shoes, and professional appearance. Casual Fridays allow jeans and polo shirts. Remote employees should maintain professional appearance during client-facing video calls. Specific departments like sales may have stricter requirements.

BENEFITS AND COMPENSATION

Q: When do benefits begin for new employees?
A: Health insurance coverage begins on the first day of the month following your start date, provided you've completed enrollment within 30 days. Dental and vision coverage follows the same timeline. 401(k) eligibility begins immediately, but company matching starts after 6 months of employment. PTO accrual begins immediately but cannot be used until after 60 days of employment.

Q: How is paid time off calculated and tracked?
A: PTO is accrued monthly based on years of service. Employees with 0-2 years receive 15 days annually (1.25 days per month), 3-5 years receive 20 days (1.67 days per month), and 6+ years receive 25 days (2.08 days per month). PTO must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance for non-emergency situations. Maximum carryover is 40 hours into the next calendar year.

Q: What health insurance plans are available?
A: We offer three health insurance options through BlueCross BlueShield: Basic PPO with $2,500 deductible (company pays 80%), Standard PPO with $1,500 deductible (company pays 85%), and Premium PPO with $500 deductible (company pays 90%). All plans include prescription coverage, mental health services, and telehealth options. Dependent coverage is available at additional cost.

Q: How does the 401(k) retirement plan work?
A: Employees can contribute up to the IRS maximum (currently $23,000 for 2024, $30,500 for those 50+). The company matches 100% of the first 3% and 50% of the next 2% of your contributions after 6 months of employment. Vesting is immediate for employee contributions and follows a 4-year graded schedule for company matches: 25% after 2 years, 50% after 3 years, 100% after 4 years.

PAYROLL AND TIMEKEEPING

Q: What are the standard pay periods and payroll schedule?
A: We operate on a bi-weekly payroll schedule with pay dates every other Friday. Time sheets must be submitted by 5 PM on Monday following the end of each pay period. Direct deposit is strongly encouraged and typically processes by 8 AM on payday. Paper checks are available but must be picked up from HR by 3 PM on payday.

Q: How do I report time for hourly employees?
A: Hourly employees must use the TimeTracker system to clock in and out. Break times of 15 minutes or less are paid and don't require clocking out. Lunch breaks of 30 minutes or more must be clocked out. Any overtime (over 40 hours per week) must be pre-approved by your supervisor and will be paid at 1.5x regular rate.

Q: What is the policy on expense reimbursements?
A: Business expenses must be pre-approved and submitted within 30 days using ExpenseTracker with original receipts. Mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS rate ($0.67/mile for 2024). Meal allowances are $25 for lunch and $50 for dinner during business travel. Hotel accommodations should be mid-range business hotels, and flight bookings should be economy class unless flights exceed 4 hours.

PERFORMANCE AND DEVELOPMENT

Q: How often are performance reviews conducted?
A: Annual performance reviews are conducted in Q4 (October-December) for all employees. New employees receive additional reviews at 90 days and 6 months. Reviews include goal-setting, competency assessments, 360-degree feedback from peers and supervisors, and development planning. Performance ratings directly impact merit increases and bonus eligibility.

Q: What professional development opportunities are available?
A: The company provides $2,000 annual professional development budget per employee for conferences, workshops, online courses, and certifications relevant to your role. Internal mentorship programs pair junior employees with senior staff. We also offer tuition reimbursement up to $5,000 annually for job-related degree programs with prior approval and minimum grade requirements.

Q: How does the promotion process work?
A: Promotions are merit-based and consider performance history, skill development, and business needs. Open positions are posted internally for 5 business days before external recruiting begins. Lateral transfers are encouraged for career development. Promotion discussions should occur during annual reviews or with your supervisor when career opportunities arise.

TECHNOLOGY AND SECURITY

Q: What equipment will be provided for my role?
A: Standard equipment includes a laptop (Windows or Mac based on role), monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset, and necessary software licenses. Remote employees receive a $500 home office setup allowance. IT equipment remains company property and must be returned upon termination. Personal use of company equipment is permitted within reasonable limits.

Q: What is the policy on software installations and IT security?
A: All software installations must be approved by IT department. Only authorized software from the approved list can be installed on company devices. Password requirements include 12+ characters with complexity requirements and must be changed every 90 days. Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all business applications and email access.

Q: How do I access company systems remotely?
A: Remote access is provided through secure VPN connection using company-issued credentials. VPN must be active when accessing any company resources from outside the office. Personal devices cannot be used to access company data without prior IT approval and security configuration. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately to IT and HR.

WORKPLACE POLICIES

Q: What is the policy on workplace harassment and discrimination?
A: We maintain zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or retaliation based on protected characteristics. All incidents should be reported to HR immediately through multiple channels: direct supervisor, HR hotline (555-0123), or anonymous ethics portal. All reports are investigated promptly and confidentially. Retaliation against reporters is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action.

Q: What are the guidelines for social media and external communications?
A: Employees should not speak on behalf of the company on social media unless authorized. Personal social media should include disclaimers that views are your own. Confidential company information should never be shared publicly. Professional networking is encouraged but should maintain appropriate boundaries regarding proprietary information and client relationships.

Q: How does the remote work policy function?
A: Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with supervisor approval. Core collaboration hours are 10 AM - 3 PM in company time zone. Home office must meet ergonomic and connectivity standards. Regular check-ins with supervisors are required, and in-person attendance for team meetings and client presentations may be mandatory.

LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE

Q: What types of leave are available beyond PTO?
A: Family and Medical Leave (FMLA) provides up to 12 weeks unpaid leave for qualifying situations. Short-term disability covers up to 26 weeks at 60% pay for medical conditions. Bereavement leave provides 3 days paid for immediate family, 1 day for extended family. Jury duty is paid leave with proof of service. Military leave follows federal guidelines with job protection.

Q: How is attendance tracked and what are the expectations?
A: Standard work hours are 8 AM - 5 PM with 1-hour lunch break. Flexible start times between 7-9 AM are permitted with supervisor approval. Tardiness is recorded after 15 minutes, and patterns of lateness will result in performance counseling. Unexcused absences may result in disciplinary action including termination.

Q: What is the sick leave policy?
A: Sick leave accrues at 4 hours per month (6 days annually) for full-time employees. Unused sick leave carries over up to 48 hours annually. Doctor's notes are required for illnesses exceeding 3 consecutive days. Sick leave can be used for personal illness, family member care, or medical appointments. Abuse of sick leave policy may result in disciplinary action.
